# Watch

> **Anticipate danger so nothing can surprise you again.**

This booklet explores a human gesture that protects, connects and supports — and may then become the only way left to answer the world.

## The human gesture

To watch is to pay attention, protect, remember what others forget and remain present when someone is vulnerable. It can be a profound form of love and responsibility.

## Its latent closure

The gesture closes when uncertainty becomes unbearable. Rest begins to resemble a fault; delegating, abandonment. Every event risks becoming a warning that everyone else still refuses to see.

## Colour of the five depths

| Depth | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| **Mask** | I notice what others miss; I warn, prepare and stay awake. |
| **Protection** | I watch exits, memorise schedules, repeat checks or always imagine the worst before it arrives. |
| **Attachment** | Someone, somewhere or a promise still depends on my vigilance. |
| **Wound** | Once I failed to see it coming; I arrived too late; no one believed me while there was still time. |
| **Inner World** | Details announce danger. As long as I keep watch, catastrophe remains suspended. |

## Ambivalences to preserve

- protecting someone / controlling them;
- perceiving a real danger / manufacturing coherence between signs;
- longing finally to sleep / trusting no one to take over.

No thread should resolve these tensions in advance.

## Fragment bank

:::note

Choosing a fragment does not fix a complete history. Its relationship with other depths will be created secretly by the player, then discovered in play.

:::

### Mask

- I know the name of everyone who comes through this door.
- I always sleep closest to the exit.
- I warn people before they even ask.
- I notice immediately what has changed.

### Protection

- I check three times what others say they already checked.
- I imagine the worst first; that way it cannot surprise me.
- I stay awake when others lower their guard.
- I always keep an exit and a witness in sight.

### Attachment

- Someone relies on me to come home alive.
- The room next door must never stay silent for too long.
- I keep the watch of the person I was supposed to wake.
- I watch over a promise no one else remembers.

### Wound

- I thought he was still asleep.
- Once, I laughed when someone asked me for help.
- Everyone said there was no danger.
- I had been entrusted with one thing, and I looked away.

### Inner World

- Accidents always warn someone before they happen.
- When every door is closed, something chooses a window.
- If I fall asleep first, tomorrow will not be the same.
- The lights flicker to count those who are missing.
