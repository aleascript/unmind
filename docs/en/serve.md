# Serve

> **Become necessary so you will not be abandoned.**

This booklet explores a human gesture that protects, connects and supports — and may then become the only way left to answer the world.

## The human gesture

To serve is to care, make shared life possible and notice a need before it is spoken. The character may be generous, capable and genuinely attentive to others.

## Its latent closure

The gesture closes when personal worth depends entirely on usefulness. The character can no longer ask, receive or simply be there. They may end up maintaining — even producing — the needs that guarantee they cannot be dispensed with.

## Colour of the five depths

| Depth | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| **Mask** | I help, repair, bring, clean or know exactly what everyone needs. |
| **Protection** | I stay busy, demand nothing and turn my own needs into tasks to perform for others. |
| **Attachment** | A person, function or place still needs me. |
| **Wound** | I was left when I was no longer useful; only what I could provide was loved; no one came when I needed help. |
| **Inner World** | If no one needs me, I stop existing for them. Every crisis is also proof that I still have a place. |

## Ambivalences to preserve

- care / control;
- generosity / disappearance of self;
- relieving a need / needing it to remain.

No thread should resolve these tensions in advance.

## Fragment bank

:::note

Choosing a fragment does not fix a complete history. Its relationship with other depths will be created secretly by the player, then discovered in play.

:::

### Mask

- I know how everyone takes their tea.
- I always have something to repair.
- I finish the tasks others abandon.
- People come looking for me when no one knows what to do.

### Protection

- I ask what needs doing before anyone can ask how I am.
- As long as my hands are busy, no one asks the wrong question.
- I anticipate needs before they can turn into reproaches.
- I never accept what I cannot immediately repay.

### Attachment

- Someone here only eats if I sit opposite them.
- My sister still sends me lists of things to do.
- I keep a place ready for the person who will come back for me.
- The plant in the corridor depends on me alone.

### Wound

- They thanked me, then replaced me.
- The only time I asked for help, no one came.
- When I could no longer care for her, she stopped looking at me.
- I learned too late that you can love someone without being able to save them.

### Inner World

- People disappear as soon as they no longer need me.
- Every broken object is a discreet way of calling me.
- If I finish every task, they will finally let me go home.
- Someone causes these small disasters to check that I am still here.
