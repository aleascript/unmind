# Resist

> **Preserve your existence through refusal and opposition.**

This booklet explores a human gesture that protects, connects and supports — and may then become the only way left to answer the world.

## The human gesture

To resist is to set a boundary, protect dignity, recognise injustice and refuse submission. It can be courage, lucidity and loyalty to what must not be negotiated.

## Its latent closure

The gesture closes when every request becomes control. Refusal then remains the only proof of existence; accepting help, changing one's mind or trusting someone resembles capitulation.

## Colour of the five depths

| Depth | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| **Mask** | I say no, challenge authority, provoke or say aloud what others keep quiet. |
| **Protection** | I refuse before I can be forced, test intentions, reject gifts and turn every relationship into an explicit power struggle. |
| **Attachment** | A person, cause, memory or promise is precisely what I refuse to betray. |
| **Wound** | My consent was ignored; an authority deceived me; giving in once cost something irreplaceable. |
| **Inner World** | Every act of obedience erases part of me. Benevolent gestures can be the most effective forms of capture. |

## Ambivalences to preserve

- courage / reflexive opposition;
- discernment / generalised suspicion;
- loyalty / isolation.

No thread should resolve these tensions in advance.

## Fragment bank

:::note

Choosing a fragment does not fix a complete history. Its relationship with other depths will be created secretly by the player, then discovered in play.

:::

### Mask

- I always ask who decided.
- I prefer punishment to permission.
- I say no before anyone can mistake my silence for agreement.
- I make every order harder to pronounce.

### Protection

- I refuse gifts whose price I did not set.
- I push people until their intention appears.
- I contradict my own desire if someone orders me to follow it.
- I keep one fault visible so they know they did not correct everything.

### Attachment

- I promised never to sign for someone else.
- Someone still believes I can say no for both of us.
- I keep the name they tried to take away from me.
- There is a door I refuse to cross without the person left behind.

### Wound

- I obeyed once and no one gave back what it cost me.
- They called care what I had asked them to stop.
- The person who was supposed to defend me explained that it was for my own good.
- I agreed in order to protect someone, and they treated my yes as permanent.

### Inner World

- Every regulation contains the sentence they are afraid to say aloud.
- Doors open only when you stop asking permission.
- Every time I say yes without resisting, someone erases a line from my history.
- The walls repeat the orders no one dares give out loud.
