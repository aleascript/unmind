# Order

> **Make the world predictable through rules, signs or rituals.**

This booklet explores a human gesture that protects, connects and supports — and may then become the only way left to answer the world.

## The human gesture

To order is to give form to chaos, preserve continuity, understand structures and create habits that make life inhabitable. It can be intelligence, care or craft.

## Its latent closure

The gesture closes when exception becomes an ontological threat. Classification replaces encounter; the constructed rule becomes confused with reality, and other people may have to submit to it so the world does not come apart.

## Colour of the five depths

| Depth | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| **Mask** | I am exact, reliable and methodical; I know where things belong and how they should happen. |
| **Protection** | I count, classify, repeat, correct or follow a sequence from which no step can be omitted. |
| **Attachment** | A ritual, responsibility, object or person still gives living meaning to the order I maintain. |
| **Wound** | Chaos had a price; an authority arbitrarily changed the rules; a single error seems to have caused the irreparable. |
| **Inner World** | The world has a hidden grammar. Certain numbers, placements or sequences keep reality from coming apart. |

## Ambivalences to preserve

- creating meaning / imposing one's coherence;
- caring / controlling;
- discovering a pattern / producing it oneself.

No thread should resolve these tensions in advance.

## Fragment bank

:::note

Choosing a fragment does not fix a complete history. Its relationship with other depths will be created secretly by the player, then discovered in play.

:::

### Mask

- I put every chair back in its place before I leave.
- I know the schedules better than the staff.
- I correct dates even when the story is true.
- Others entrust me with what they do not want to lose.

### Protection

- I count to a number that divides nothing.
- I start again whenever a step is interrupted.
- I classify what happens to me before deciding what I feel about it.
- I never let two identical objects touch.

### Attachment

- I keep the table as it was when there were still four of us.
- Someone agrees to follow my sequence without mocking me.
- This watch always stops at the hour when we are supposed to think of him.
- There is a rule I promised never to break.

### Wound

- They changed the rule after I obeyed it.
- One mistake from me was enough.
- That day nothing was in its place and no one wanted to listen.
- I threw away what seemed useless before I understood what it was.

### Inner World

- Odd numbers warn when someone is going to lie.
- A badly ordered room attracts events that resemble it.
- If the sequence is complete, the day starts again without the accident.
- The building changes its plan when you stop counting its doors.
